ABOUT DSB BOREWELLS

Our Story

DSB Borewells is a trusted name in borewell drilling and groundwater discovery, providing reliable and efficient water solutions for residential, agricultural and industrial needs.

With over 42+ years of experience and a commitment to quality, we have successfully completed 5200+ projects across all over Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and neighbouring states. Our focus is on delivering sustainable water solutions using precision groundwater finding surveys, modern Ashok Leyland drilling technology, and skilled operators.
